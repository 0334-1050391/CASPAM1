Introduction

The Computer Science Department was established to provide excellence in teaching, research, and innovation in computing technologies.

Vision

  • To be a center of excellence in computing education.
  • To produce globally competitive graduates.
  • To promote research and innovation.

Mission

  1. Provide quality education.
  2. Encourage research activities.
  3. Develop industry collaborations.

Chairperson's Message

"Our goal is to empower students with knowledge and skills to meet global technological challenges."
